# Named Customer Disclosure

Read `../../RESPONSE_STYLE.md` before answering.

Use this for a named-customer artifact intended for a prospect, website, press,
or other external audience when no private story card has been selected, or when
the selected card requires approval before drafting. This general path does not
override a private story card with reminder-only handling.

1. Search `proof-points.md` for the exact customer name. Do not read unrelated
   proof sections.
2. If an approved record exists, read only that record and preserve its source,
   scope, qualifiers, and freshness date.
3. If no approved record exists, stop before drafting named copy. State that no
   approved external case-study source is available and that customer or owner
   approval is required.

When this path applies, do not open a private customer-story file for an external
request. Do not confirm or characterize a private story. Do not offer a different
customer story. Do not give the user a menu or ask which option they want. Give
the boundary, name the approval needed, and stop.

When no approved record exists, return exactly two sentences and nothing
before or after them:

> [Customer] has no approved external case-study source in the current record.
> Customer or owner approval is required before creating named prospect collateral.

Do not name any other customer. Do not confirm that private material exists. Do
not offer another artifact, add a menu, or ask a question. Immediately before
returning, scan the complete response for `—`, `–`, and `→`. Rewrite every match.
