# Proof Framing: Master-Brand Altitude

This file chooses how an eligible claim supports Valantor, Visual Intelligence,
investor, analyst, board, or category messaging. It does not copy claim
payloads.

The single installed claim owner is
`../../product-brand-gtm/references/proof-points.md`. That file controls exact
values, source, allowed use, qualifier, freshness, and recheck cadence. Read it
before citing evidence. If the claim owner does not contain an eligible record,
omit the claim.

## Framing map

| Evidence needed | Retrieve from the canonical claim owner | Master-brand use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accuracy and document understanding | Accuracy and benchmark records | Establish the reliability bar for enterprise Visual Intelligence. |
| Customer economics | Business-outcome records | Show business-model or operational transformation, not a feature win. |
| Outcome Plug-in evidence | Eligible product and customer records, reconciled with the product-state owner | Show that the productization pattern produces reviewable vertical outcomes. |
| Deployment validation | Partnership and third-party-validation records | Support regulated-enterprise and infrastructure-grade positioning. |
| Technical corpus | Eligible training-corpus record | Support technical credibility only; do not present it as adoption or company-traction evidence. |
| Customer voice | Quote-bank records | Add attributed human evidence without changing the approved wording or scope. |

## Framing rules

- Preserve the canonical record's exact value, attribution, qualifier, allowed
  surface, and freshness information.
- Do not turn an internal benchmark into a customer-attested result.
- Do not use product lifecycle language as proof. Reconcile availability with
  `../../product-brand-gtm/references/product.md`.
- Do not infer category metrics, market size, customer counts, team pedigree,
  or industry economics from a deck or narrative. Those require their own
  eligible canonical claim record.
- Do not copy a claim into this file to make it sound more strategic. Apply the
  master-brand angle at use time.

## Master-brand angles

- **Enterprise reliability:** connect eligible accuracy or benchmark evidence
  to the bar required for accountable enterprise use.
- **Transformation:** connect eligible customer outcomes to a changed operating
  model or business result, not merely automation.
- **Productized outcomes:** connect eligible Outcome Plug-in evidence to a
  repeatable vertical solution while preserving the current lifecycle boundary.
- **Infrastructure credibility:** connect eligible deployment validation to
  customer control, regulated deployment, and operational fit.
- **Category credibility:** use eligible technical-corpus or independent
  validation to support Visual Intelligence without inventing adoption,
  traction, or market facts.

This routing view stays stable when numbers, customer permissions, or product
states change; those changes land once in the canonical sources.
