# People (master-brand altitude)

Reference material for Valantor's named leadership team, drawn from company
bios and independent research. Select and adapt the relevant details for the
audience and format.

## 1. Using this reference

Each executive's section has two parts:

- **Company profile**: a concise summary based on valantor.com.
- **Additional background**: details gathered from independent research.

These are drafting inputs, not fixed copy. Current roles and time-sensitive
details may need verification before external use.

## 2. Patrick McCamley — Chief Executive Officer

**Company profile.** A commercial executive with a track
record of scaling enterprise businesses across technology, energy, and
infrastructure, including President and Chief Commercial Officer roles at
Arcadia, Vistra, and Siemens.

**Additional background.**
- Co-founder of Valantor, alongside Chaitu Parikh.
- At Arcadia, a clean-energy technology company, held Chief Commercial
  Officer (2020) then President (2022-2025), immediately before joining
  Valantor.
- Valantor's Chief Executive Officer since July 2026.

## 3. Chaitu Parikh — Chief Operating Officer

**Company profile.** A 25-year veteran of the energy
sector with a track record of driving growth and operational efficiency,
including President and Head of Retail Energy at David Energy and COO at
Crius Energy, where he helped deliver a 250% increase in customers.

**Additional background.**
- Co-founder of Valantor, alongside Patrick McCamley.
- President and Head of Retail Energy at David Energy, January 2021 to
  February 2025.
- Chief Operating Officer at Crius Energy, August 2013 to October 2018.
- Roughly nine and a half years at MXenergy: Executive Vice President and
  Chief Financial Officer (2002-2011), then President (2011-2012), through
  its sale to Constellation.
- University of Toronto, Bachelor of Arts in Economics, Accounting, and
  Business.
- Valantor's Chief Operating Officer since July 2025.

## 4. Ben Fletcher — Chief Technology Officer

**Company profile.** Designed quantum computing chips at
IBM Research, built consumer AI at IBM Watson, and engineered the Weather
Channel's conversational platform. Holder of 21 patents and a Ph.D. in
physics. An early OpenAI beta user.

**Additional background.**
- Co-founded EyeLevel.ai in 2018 and served as its Chief Executive Officer,
  November 2018 to March 2026; became Valantor's Chief Technology Officer
  in March 2026 after Valantor's acquisition of EyeLevel.
- Holds 21 U.S. patents listed individually under his own name, spanning
  file-system, document-annotation, and related software and security
  inventions.
- Ph.D. in Materials Science and Engineering, University of Tennessee,
  Knoxville (2004-2008); Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering, same
  institution (1998-2004).
- Senior Technical Product Manager, IBM Watson, February 2015 to April
  2016: a founding member of IBM Watson Life, an internal incubator for
  consumer AI products.
- Director of Product Management, The Weather Company, November 2016 to
  December 2017: led product for the team behind the Weather Channel's
  2016 Facebook Messenger chatbot.

## 5. Neil Katz — Chief Product Officer

**Company profile.** Led product and content at IBM
Watson's Weather division, serving 200M+ global users. 4X Emmy-winning
executive producer. Founded Meteor, an AI media brand. Columbia MS, NYU
MBA.

**Additional background.**
- Co-Founder and Chief Operating Officer of EyeLevel.ai, February 2023 to
  July 2026: recapitalized the company, led product, marketing, and sales,
  brought in Air France, ADP, and Samsung as customers, and launched GroundX,
  FraudX, and X-Ray. Became
  Valantor's Chief Product Officer in July 2026, after Valantor's
  acquisition of EyeLevel.
- Global Head of Subscription Weather Products, IBM Watson, January 2018 to
  September 2020: led product and content across Weather.com, Weather
  Underground, and subscription products, reaching 200M users.
- Senior Vice President, Global Content and Editor in Chief, The Weather
  Channel, November 2015 to January 2018: 4 Emmy Awards, 2 Edward R. Murrow
  Awards, a George Polk Award, an IRE Award, and 60+ other industry awards.
- Earlier journalism career in brief: senior editorial roles at
  CBSNews.com and The Huffington Post, and earlier field and video
  journalism at CBS News and The New York Times.
